## Main Features
<a id="main-features"></a>

### Core Functionality
- **Multimedia Playback** — Play any multimedia content stored locally, imported, or streamed via iTunes.
- **Device Synchronization** — Synchronize the multimedia library of iPhones, iPods, and iPads seamlessly with your Windows PC.
- **Library Management** — Catalog multimedia files, organizing them into folders, while obtaining and editing song information efficiently.
- **CD Importing** — Import songs from CDs in various formats, including AIFF, AAC, MP3, WAV, HE-AAC, or Apple Lossless.
- **CD Burning** — Burn CDs or create backups of your media library effortlessly.

### Advanced Capabilities
- **Live Radio Streaming** — Listen to live radio broadcasts sorted by genre, including access to Beats 1, Apple's exclusive radio station.
- **Apple Music Integration** — Access Apple Music to stream millions of songs without the need to download them.
- **Genius Functionality** — Utilize the Genius feature to search your song library and create customized mixes, generating a personalized radio experience.
- **iTunes Store Access** — Purchase individual songs or full albums from the expansive iTunes Store, along with access to movies and documentaries available for rent or purchase.
